# Explainability & Governance Statement

## Decision Architecture
GitContractShield reviews contracts through deterministic clause extraction and legal policy heuristics. When liability sections are evaluated, the agent calculates the ratio of the proposed liability cap against the total contract value. If an uncapped indemnity clause or non-approved governing jurisdiction is identified, the agent generates an automated redline alternative with clause citations.

## Input Data Provenance
Data inputs include legal agreements in markdown/text format, commercial terms schedules, and corporate standard negotiation playbooks.

## Operational Limits & Non-Goals
GitContractShield provides legal risk assessment and drafting suggestions; it does not constitute formal legal counsel or represent clients in formal judicial proceedings.
